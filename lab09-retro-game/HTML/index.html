A Little About Me

I’m not a huge gamer. I grew up in a time when most kids spent their summer days outside at the park from dawn until dusk. There were these boys who lived across the street from us who we used to play with. They made us cry sometimes, but that’s another story.

One day, they invited my sister and me over to play a video game. The game was called Mario Party.

This video game was unlike anything I had ever seen before. It was so vibrant and 3D! At the time, I had never seen a video game like that. And one of the characters was a princess!

Yes, please!

I got to be the princess, Peach, while my sister got to be Yoshi. The second-closest thing to a girl character.

Donkey Kong 64

One of the first games I remember playing on my own. I loved exploring the world, collecting bananas, and unlocking the different Kongs. Lanky Kong was my favourite because, honestly, how could you not love that goofy-looking Kong and his ridiculously long arms?

Super Mario 64

Super Mario 64 was one of the games that made me realize how much I loved exploring 3D worlds. Running around the castle, jumping into paintings, and discovering new areas made the game feel like a huge adventure. It was a lot more fun for me than what I like to call "jump and die" games.
