Reviews

Donkey Kong 64

Donkey Kong 64 was one of the first games that I remember playing on my own. I loved exploring the different areas of the world, collecting bananas, finding secrets, and, of course, saving the Kongs. One of my favourite parts of the game was unlocking a new Kong and discovering their unique abilities. Each character felt different, which made exploring the world even more fun because I was always wondering what I would be able to access next. Lanky Kong was definitely my favourite. There was just something about that goofy-looking Kong and his long arms that made him my favourite character to play.

I also really enjoyed the music in this game. And I have to talk about the opening rap. It was so good. Hilarious, weird, and somehow completely unforgettable. I don't think anyone could make an opening like that today and get away with it. Donkey Kong 64 is one of those games that brings back a lot of childhood memories for me, and I still have a soft spot for it because of that.

Super Mario 64

Super Mario 64 was another single-player game that I really enjoyed. What I loved most was being able to explore a 3D world. Before playing games like this, I never really understood what made video games so fun. Going left or right on a flat screen never really interested me. I like to call those games "jump and die" games because that is basically all that happens to me when I play them.

Super Mario 64 completely changed that for me. I loved being able to run around, jump wherever I wanted, explore different areas, and discover things for myself. The castle was especially fun because it felt like there was always another room or secret to find. It was one of those games where I could spend a lot of time just wandering around and seeing what I could find.

Looking back, I think Super Mario 64 is one of the games that helped me realize that I really enjoy 3D games. It made exploring part of the fun instead of just something you had to do to get to the next level.

Mario Party 64

I believe Mario Party was the first video game I ever played. What I remember most is how much I enjoyed playing together with other people. Taking turns, competing against each other, and seeing who would come out on top made it feel like something the whole family could enjoy.

I also loved that Mario Party didn't feel too much like a "boy game" to me. I could just pick a character, play the mini-games, and have fun without feeling like the game wasn't meant for me. There was something really fun about sitting together and playing, even when everyone was trying to sabotage each other.

Honestly, I don't remember much about the actual gameplay anymore. What I do remember is the blister I somehow ended up with right in the centre of my palm. If you've played Mario Party, you probably already know exactly what happened. Those old mini-games could get surprisingly competitive! Even though my memories of the individual games are fuzzy, Mario Party still stands out to me because it reminds me of playing video games with my family.

GoldenEye 007

GoldenEye 007 was another fun multiplayer game that I remember playing with my family. I loved exploring the levels and looking around to see what I could find. The only problem was that my older siblings were usually much more interested in shooting me than letting me enjoy my little adventure.

I honestly spent more time exploring the levels than I did trying to win. There was something really fun about running around and discovering different areas, even when I knew I was probably about to get shot by one of my siblings. The multiplayer made the game even more memorable because it turned it into something we could all play together and compete against each other.

GoldenEye is one of those N64 games that I remember less for how good I actually was at it and more for the memories of playing it with my family. Getting destroyed by my older siblings was apparently part of the experience.
