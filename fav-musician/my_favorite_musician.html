About Michael

Michael Jackson's music style is unique and diverse. He is often called the "king of pop". However, his music consists of a variety of different styles, including pop, R&B, rock, soul, and disco. He is my favourite musician because he has a lot of great songs, his voice is beautiful, and he's a great dancer. I also admire how kind and gentle he appeared to be in the recorded interviews and interactions I've seen. I like different types of music, so depending on what mood I'm in, Michael has a song that.

Michael Jackson began singing when he was a child and was the lead singer in his family band called the Jackson 5. The Jackson 5 became an instant sensation. They signed a record deal with Motown Records in 1969, their debut single "I Want You Back" went to number one on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1970. The Jackson 5 was created by Michael's father, Joseph Jackson, and included his older brothers. As Michael grew older, he eventually separated professionally from his father and began building his own identity as a solo artist.
