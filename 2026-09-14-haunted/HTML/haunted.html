Lost Spirit

It was a dark and stormy night...

Just kidding. It was actually a beautiful summer evening.

The kind of night where nothing bad is supposed to happen.

You're home alone, sitting on the couch, when an uncomfortable feeling begins creeping over you. You try to ignore it, but you can't shake the feeling that someone is watching you.

Then you see it.

Out of the corner of your eye, a black silhouette is standing at the top of the stairs.

You turn your head.

Nothing is there.

You tell yourself you're imagining things.

Then you hear a floorboard creak upstairs.

You are not alone.

Whatever is inside your house knows you're here.

Make your choices carefully... or don't. You'll make it to the end either way.
