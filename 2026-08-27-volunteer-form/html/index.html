Awareness, Remembrance & Action

Remembering, Sharing, & Connecting is a community gathering created to provide a welcoming space for people in Lethbridge to come together, share stories, and enjoy memories, enjoy food, and learn about ways to support MMIWG2S+ families and communities.

What to Expect

Share Stories

A welcoming space for people who wish to share memories, stories, poetry, or artwork.

Gather Together

Enjoy food and refreshments while connecting with community members.

Take Action

Learn about ways to support MMIWG2S+ and contribute to positive change.
