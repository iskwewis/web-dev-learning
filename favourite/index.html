My Favourite Things.

My favourite hobbies are playing video games, spending time with family, and going for a walks while listening to music. I like video games because I'm able to turn my brain off and focus on one thing. One game I've been playing a lot recently is Elden Ring, but my favourite game is Skyrim. I'm not very good at Elden Ring and need my son to help me with some of the bosses.

Another way I'm able to turn off my brain is with music and walks. I always feel better after listening to my favourite tunes and getting some sunshine.

When I'm not playing video games or going for walks, I also enjoy spending time with my family. I haven't spent a lot of time with my family over the past few years. After losing my mother, I was filled filled with so much regret about the time I didn't spend with her. Since then, I've been talking and visiting with my family a lot more.

This is special.
